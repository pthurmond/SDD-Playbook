# Problem Statement: Community Garden and Seed Library

Status: Draft
Owner: Example Maintainer

## Problem

A community garden shares limited plots, seeds, tools, watering work, and volunteer time. People need to know what is available, what rules apply, whose turn it is, and what happens when plans change. A spreadsheet can start the job. It does not gracefully explain a double-booked plot, an accessibility accommodation, or a seed loan that never came back.

## Product Goal

Make shared garden resources and responsibilities visible enough that members can participate without a coordinator becoming the permanent human queue.

## Primary Journey

A member finds an available seed or plot, sees the applicable seasonal and participation rules, reserves it or joins a watering rotation, and receives a clear confirmation or explanation of why it is unavailable.

## Constraints

- Fictional teaching material, not horticultural, legal, accessibility, or municipal policy advice.
- Start with one garden site and one growing season.
- Keep weather, payments, maps, and identity-provider integration out of the first version.
- Treat accessibility needs as a respectful participation requirement, not a special-case afterthought.

## Non-Goals

- Providing planting or pesticide advice.
- Predicting crop yield.
- Managing every community program, event, donation, or grant.
- Replacing local coordinators' judgment about conflict or safety.
